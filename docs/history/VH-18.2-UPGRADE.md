Base: 18.1.0 "TeamEvolve" (cross-user team learning, unanimous consent, 102
specialists). 18.2.0 ships the three things the two external reviews and the
product owner put on the table: a premium minimal UI, the signed collaboration
invitation lifecycle the reviews named the biggest remaining gap, and bounded
recursive self-evolution. Protocol unchanged at v0.10.7.

## 2 · Signed collaboration invitations — the reviewer's biggest gap, closed

`src/vh19/collabInvite.ts`: each member has an ECDSA P-256 VH identity
(TOFU — first contact trusts the presented key and says so; binding to the A2A
identity is the honest next step, not claimed now). User 1 creates an
invitation naming scope, risk ceiling, duration and capabilities; it serializes
to a token signed over the canonical bytes. User 2 pastes it: a tampered
ceiling, a foreign key, or garbage all refuse in words. Approve/reject is a
SIGNATURE by the approver's own key, and `approveTeamEvolution` now accepts
`signedApprovals` — every approver's signature is verified, member-matched,
and consistency-checked before a team configuration can be adopted.
`probe/collabInvite` (14 checks) pins the whole lifecycle, including that
Member A's key cannot sign Qwen's consent. The door wires both directions
(create → copy token; paste → verify → sign).

## 3 · Recursive self-evolution — bounded, and the floor is the feature

The honest reading of "recursive self-evolving" for an accountability product:
VH proposes changes to its own control plane from YOUR ledger — a specialist
you keep rejecting gets a tier-tighten proposal; rejection clusters while the
bar is loose propose raising it; and rejecting three proposals in one category
suppresses that category's proposals (the recursion: it evolves its own
proposing). Every proposal is stated in words with your own numbers, applied
only by your explicit click, receipt-linked and revert-exact. What it can
NEVER do is stated and enforced: the receipt protocol, the human gate + 90%
exam, the honesty contract, and any LOOSENING — the store's shape makes a
loosening unrepresentable, and `floorIntact()` audits it. `probe/selfEvolve`
(18 checks) pins silence-before-evidence, tighten-only, revert-exactness,
bar monotonicity, the recursion and the floor. The door gets a Self-evolution
panel (propose / apply / reject / revert).

## 4 · The team self-evolves after connection

`autoProposeIfReady(teamId, members)` (wired into the door's team refresh and
after peer-delegated runs in `askVH19`): once a connected team clears the real
bar (≥3 runs, ≥1 verified, ≥2 distinct proven specialists) VH mints the
evolution proposal AUTOMATICALLY — visibly pending, never silently adopted,
never proposed twice. Adoption still needs every member's approval, now
optionally SIGNED (see §2). `probe/teamEvolve` grows to 35 checks. Grounding:
Swarm-Skills-style auto-distillation with human review for structural changes
(arXiv 2605.10052) and Meta-Team team-scaffold commits (arXiv 2605.29790) —
auto-PROPOSE, human-APPROVE.

# Vouch Harbor 18.5.0 "Apex" — upgrade notes

Base: 18.4.0 "Zenith". 18.5.0 brings the mechanics the 2026 always-on-agent
category made famous — goal mode, standing approval rules, checkpointed
resumable work — rebuilt on VH's invariants: local-first, receipt-everything,
human gate supreme, no fake execution, no new framework, and no cloud
credential surface. Research into the category stays in the project's private
records; the product speaks only its own language. Protocol unchanged at
v0.10.7.

## The shape VH takes, and the trust model it refuses

The category's cloud products give each agent a persistent remote computer
that signs into your tools and keeps working while you're away — and their
own documentation admits the per-agent computers are not security boundaries
and the credentials live on vendor cloud. The local-first open-source shapes
put the same mechanics on your machine. VH takes the mechanics from the
latter shape and refuses the former trust model:

  • goal mode, but steps settle only via the real pipeline (executed /
    planned / gated / refused — never "completed" by fiat);
  • standing approval rules, but session-only, logged, revocable, and
    powerless over `critical` work — permanent autonomy still needs the 90%
    exam;
  • checkpointed resumable work, but on YOUR disk — no remote VM, no shared
    credential surface, nothing signs into anything on your behalf.

## 1 · Assignments — goal mode with checkpoints

`src/vh19/goals.ts` + the door's Assignments panel. A goal is decomposed by
the SAME router that fields every message (≤5 specialist steps — a goal is
not a wishlist). "Run next step" pushes the step through `askVH19`: with a
provider key it executes; without one it lands as an honest PLAN; risky
steps pause at the human gate; a denied step is `refused`, a paused goal is
`paused` until an explicit Resume. Every settled step carries its
provenance digest; progress is checkpointed so a closed app resumes where it
stopped; settled steps cannot be re-settled (no outcome laundering).
`probe/goals` pins all of it (19 checks).

## 2 · Session Auto-Review rules at the gate

`src/vh19/gateRules.ts`: at a risky gate ask the modal offers "allow this
category for this session". Rules live in module memory — a restart forgets
everything; a rule answers ONLY when every category in the ask is ruled;
`critical` always pauses at a human; every rule answer is logged and shown
in the door with a one-click revoke. The engine still calls the gate; the
rule is a human-standing decision that answers it, not a bypass.

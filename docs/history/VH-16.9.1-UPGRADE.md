# VH 16.9.1 — the executive-surface release (chat-first face + the three unifying planes)

*Vouch Harbor 16.9.1 — the accountable agent OS (govern · execute · verify · learn).*

16.9.1 keeps the proven 16.9 engine byte-for-byte in its governed paths and
rebuilds the PRODUCT SURFACE around it: the product now opens as a chat —
the ChatGPT/Claude posture — with the machinery one deliberate click away,
and the deep 16.x machinery is unified into the three questions a buyer, an
auditor and an investor actually ask.

## What 16.9.1 adds over 16.9.0

- **THE CHAT-FIRST FACE** — `src/vouch/pages/VouchPage.tsx` restyled as the
  product's front: one centered conversation column (760px), a Doto hero
  wordmark, four starter cards (Live fact · Compute · Act (gated) · Dispatch
  a mission), Claude-style message rows, and a calm composer with a
  QUICK / DEEP ⚡ segmented cycle-depth control. Every legacy hardcoded hex
  is gone from the page — tokens only. The MEMORY/missions/receipts rail is
  now a drawer (default closed, opened by the header "Panel" button), and
  the App rail leads with a "+ New chat" hero button; the other doors become
  icon-only machinery items that reveal labels on hover. No engine path
  changed — the one-throat rule holds.
- **THE THREE UNIFYING PLANES** — `src/panels/ExecutivePanels.tsx`
  (+ the new `probe/executivePanels.test.ts`, 92nd live suite):
  1. **Trust Center** (Proof door) — unifies the receipt vault, the Ed25519
     issuer posture, the assurance score and the EU AI Act / ISO 42001 /
     SOC 2 control crosswalk into one diligence surface with a one-click
     **Trust Pack** export naming the zero-install verifier.
  2. **Assurance Scorecard** (Audit door) — the 0–100 measured-only
     assurance score, its band, and evidence coverage, with dilution
     stated honestly (simulated runs dilute, never add).
  3. **Cost per Outcome** (Audit door) — measured chargeback from the
     budget ledger: tokens-only seats stay `unmeasured` (never priced),
     simulated seats are counted, never charged; digest-stamped CSV export.
  The web edition renders these on **labeled demo state** (badged inline,
  per the repo's Web = labeled demo contract); the desktop edition derives
  the same panels from live local records.
- **THE QUIET FOUR** — the six-palette INK set retires for four themes
  designed dark-first, one signal colour each, colour = meaning:
  **obsidian** (soft-jet dark, smoked apricot — the flagship),
  **graphite** (cool dark, whetsteel), **porcelain** (warm white light,
  deep apricot), **bone** (deep paper light, bronze-olive). All 111 shared
  selector groups migrated on the same 56-token color-mix contract; old
  saved preferences migrate deliberately (ink/pitch→obsidian,
  slag/fern→graphite, ivory→porcelain, travertine→bone); `theme.test`
  re-pins the set (65/65). A premium motion layer (tactile buttons, message
  entrance, typing pulse, nav rail indicator, composer focus ring) rides
  transform/opacity only and honours `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **OFFLINE-HONESTY** (carried from the 16.9.0 audit) — the two tools that
  rebuild the engine bundle REFUSE IN WORDS without `node_modules`
  (`REFUSED (needs node_modules)`, exit 2) and the offline runner marks
  those bundles `SKIP (needs node_modules)` — counted separately, named in
  the summary, never laundered into passes; exit stays failure-only for
  real failures.
- **THE RELEASE-IDENTITY HOLE IS CLOSED** — the 16.9.1 external review
  found `verify/BUILD-INFO.txt` still opening as **16.8.0** with no gate
  rejecting it. `versionDrift` now pins the file's opening identity to
  `VH_VERSION`; BUILD-INFO is regenerated for this release. All version
  stamps agree at 16.9.1 (package.json, package-lock, src/version.ts,
  Cargo.toml, Cargo.lock, tauri.conf.json), and the byte-pinned MCP engine
  bundle was rebuilt + re-sealed through the official recipe.
- **HONESTY AT THE ROOT** — rendering the crosswalk on the visible surface
  exposed the last heritage name (`whatMJProvides` and two "VH" strings in
  the control copy) that the 16.5 clean-identity pass had missed; renamed
  to `whatVhProvides` at the engine, co-pinned in `evidencePack.test`.

## What did NOT change

- The governed pipeline: Vouch control plane → governed executor → mission
  loop → receipts → genome → MCP. No new MCP tools, no wire change; the
  20-tool dual-era surface and its official-SDK conformance pins stand.
- The receipt protocol, the human gate, the one-throat engine rule.

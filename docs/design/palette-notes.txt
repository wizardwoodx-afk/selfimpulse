5 colors for your agentic system (genuinely uncommon names)

🌑 Dark theme #1 — Feldgrau

#4D5D53 — a muted gray-green, originally the German military uniform color. Almost never seen in tech UI, reads as serious and grounded without being another gray-blue.

🌑 Dark theme #2 — Sinopia

#CB410B-toned dark base, but used desaturated for backgrounds — actually better as accent (see below). For the second dark surface, use Bistre 
#3D2B1F, a dark yellowish-brown made historically from wood soot — gives warmth instead of the cold blue-black everyone defaults to.

☀️ Light theme #1 — Isabelline

#F4F0EC — a pale grayish-yellow-beige, named after a (probably apocryphal) story about a Spanish queen's underwear. Distinct from "off-white" or "bone."

☀️ Light theme #2 — Eburnean

#EFDFBB-adjacent ivory tone, desaturated to 
#E8E2D0 for surfaces — literally "ivory-like," rarely used as a design-system name.

⭐ The rare accent — Skobeloff

#007474 — a genuinely obscure deep cyan-teal (comes from a discontinued Crayola color). This is your best bet for "rare AND unique" since it's barely used anywhere in design systems, unlike verdigris/malachite which have gotten more common in "unique AI palette" prompts recently.

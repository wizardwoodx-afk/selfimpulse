# Demo data notice — synthetic industrial finance demo

**Status: deterministic synthetic fixture.**

The FY 2025-26 ledger shipped under `demo/` (RAW + CLASSIFIED workbooks, SUMMARY,
RECEIPT) names **Synthetic Industrial Ltd** (signatory: A. Raghavan, GM Finance) as
the demo of record. The company, its signatory and every counterparty are invented.

All 5,200 ledger rows are **deterministically synthetic** — generated by
`tools/make-finance-demo.mjs` from a fixed seed (anchors + pseudo-random fill). They
contain no real customer, vendor, salary or banking data. Dates, parties, voucher
numbers and amounts are invented; any resemblance to real transactions is coincidental
by construction (the generator produces them from arithmetic, not from records).

If this archive is redistributed outside the original evaluation context, keep this
notice alongside `demo/`, or replace the demo artifacts with your own workbook — the
finance pipeline (`probe/financeExcel.test.ts`) classifies any sheet in the same shape.
